description: Analyze the published hackathon challenge and judging rules; choose its MVP, architecture and delivery plan.
---

Read docs/challenge.md, rules.md and evaluation.md. If the sources are missing, record gaps and request them instead of inventing requirements.

First check whether prebuilt code and AI assistance are allowed. Map actual criteria to observable acceptance scenarios. Choose the smallest coherent stack for the challenge; infra/probe is disposable, not a product-stack decision.

Update architecture.md and decisions.md with the material tradeoffs. Fill implementation-plan.md with a vertical MVP slice, dependencies, acceptance checks and demo preparation. Prioritize the complete jury scenario. Ask only decisions affecting delivery.
